The challenge

Sporadic posting; no connection between content and bookings.

The insight

The team had great moments but no system to capture them.

Strategy

Four content pillars, a weekly 30-minute shoot routine, hooks library, booking link in every post.

Execution

Shot lists, caption templates, monthly review.

Creative

Behind-the-pass moments, dish reveals, staff voices.

Channels

Instagram Reels · Google Business posts

What we would measure

  • Saves and shares
  • Profile to booking clicks
  • Posting consistency

Lesson: A system the team can keep beats a campaign they cannot.
