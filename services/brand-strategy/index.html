Positioning — explained simply

Positioning is the specific place you want to own in your customer's mind — who you're for, what you're best at, and why you instead of alternatives.

Example: 'Painless dentistry for anxious adults in West Delhi' is sharper than 'Quality dental care'.

Clear positioning makes every ad, page and conversation easier and cheaper.

  • Formula: For [audience] who [problem], we are the [category] that [unique benefit], unlike [alternative] because [proof].
  • Validate with customer language — read reviews and sales-call notes.

Common mistake: Trying to be for everyone; using generic claims like 'best quality, best price'.

Branding — explained simply

Your brand is what people feel and remember about you; branding is shaping that deliberately — name, promise, voice, visuals and experience.

Example: A ghee brand that consistently shows its farm, process and people builds trust that a discount ad never could.

Brand lowers your cost of persuasion over time: people search for you by name, trust faster and pay more.

  • Foundations: positioning, promise, personality, proof points, visual identity, tone of voice.
  • Consistency across touchpoints compounds recognition.
  • Brand + performance work together: brand builds future demand, performance captures today's.

Common mistake: Thinking branding = a logo, or changing your look every few months.
