Landing pages — explained simply

A landing page is a focused page built for one audience and one action, usually where ad traffic lands.

Example: Ad promises 'Free smile consultation in Dwarka' → page headline repeats it, shows doctor, prices from, reviews, before/after (with consent), FAQ, and a 3-field form + WhatsApp.

Message match (ad promise = page headline), one goal, proof near the CTA, fast mobile load.

  • Above the fold: who it's for, the outcome, proof, CTA.
  • Reduce friction: fewer fields, autofill, click-to-call/WhatsApp.
  • Handle objections: price, time, trust, risk (guarantee/refund policy where genuine).

Common mistake: Sending paid traffic to a homepage with 12 menu options.

Conversion rate — explained simply

Conversion rate is the % of people who take the action you want (enquire, buy, book).

Formula: Conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors (or clicks/leads) × 100

Example: 2,000 visitors → 50 enquiries → 2.5% conversion rate.

Doubling conversion rate halves your cost per lead — it's often the cheapest growth lever before buying more traffic.

  • Segment by device, source and landing page — averages hide the leak.
  • Common lifts: clearer headline, proof near the CTA, fewer form fields, faster mobile load, WhatsApp/call options, risk reversal.

Common mistake: Comparing conversion rates without stating the base (visitors vs clicks vs leads) or across very different traffic sources.
