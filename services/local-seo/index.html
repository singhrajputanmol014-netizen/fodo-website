Local SEO & Google Business Profile — explained simply

Local SEO helps you show up when nearby people search — especially in Google Maps and the 'map pack'.

Example: A salon in Rohini with a complete Google Business Profile, accurate category, real photos, steady fresh reviews and replies, plus a locality page on its website, starts showing for 'salon near me' in that area.

Ranking factors are broadly relevance (category, services), distance and prominence (reviews, links, mentions). You control relevance and prominence.

  • GBP checklist: primary + secondary categories, services, hours, products, Q&A, weekly posts/photos, booking/WhatsApp link.
  • Ask every happy customer for a review with a direct link; reply to all reviews, including negative ones, calmly.
  • Build locality/service pages on your site and consistent citations (Justdial, Practo, Sulekha, etc. as relevant).

Common mistake: Fake reviews or keyword-stuffed business names (risk of suspension), inconsistent name/address/phone across listings, ignoring review replies.

Reviews & reputation — explained simply

Reviews are social proof — they influence local rankings, conversion rates and trust.

Example: Ask every happy customer with a direct review link via WhatsApp right after service; reply to every review within 48 hours.

Steady, recent, detailed reviews beat a burst of 50 in one week.

  • Negative review reply: thank, acknowledge, take offline, fix, follow up publicly if resolved.
  • Show reviews on your landing pages near the CTA.

Common mistake: Fake or incentivised reviews (against platform rules), arguing with negative reviewers.
