Copywriting — explained simply

Copywriting is the words that make people understand, believe and act.

Example: Weak: 'Best gym in Noida.' Strong: 'Lose your first 4 kg in 6 weeks — coached, not left alone. First week free.'

Specific beats clever. Lead with the customer's outcome, add proof, remove risk, make the next step obvious.

  • Frameworks: PAS (problem-agitate-solve), AIDA, Before-After-Bridge.
  • Steal language from reviews and customer calls.

Common mistake: Talking about yourself ('we are passionate…') instead of the customer.

Offers — explained simply

Your offer is what the customer gets, for what price, with what risk — it often matters more than the ad itself.

Example: 'Book a ₹299 skin consultation, fully adjustable against treatment' beats 'Best skin clinic in Delhi'.

Strong offers are specific, valuable, low-risk and easy to say yes to.

  • Offer levers: bonus, bundle, guarantee, payment plan, speed, exclusivity, trial, free first step.
  • Match the offer to intent: cold audiences need a low-commitment first step; warm audiences can take the main offer.

Common mistake: Defaulting to discounts — they train customers to wait and erode margin.
