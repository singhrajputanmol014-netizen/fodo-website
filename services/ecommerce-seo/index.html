E-commerce & D2C growth — explained simply

E-commerce growth is a loop: product/offer → creative → traffic → product page → checkout → delivery → retention.

Example: A skincare D2C brand fixes product pages (benefits, ingredients, reviews, before/after with consent), adds COD verification, tests 6 UGC creatives on Meta and sets up cart-abandon WhatsApp + email.

Track contribution margin, CAC, conversion rate, AOV, RTO and repeat rate — not ROAS alone.

  • Shopify basics: fast theme, clear PDP, reviews app, trust badges, simple checkout, WhatsApp support.
  • Indian specifics: COD confirmation, pin-code delivery estimates, UPI, RTO reduction.
  • Retention: post-purchase flows, subscriptions/replenishment, loyalty.

Common mistake: Scaling ads before product pages convert, ignoring RTO on COD orders, discount addiction.

Keyword research & search intent — explained simply

Keyword research finds the words people actually type; search intent is the goal behind those words.

Example: 'what is invisalign' (learn) needs an explainer; 'invisalign cost delhi' (compare) needs a pricing page; 'invisalign dentist near me' (buy, local) needs a service page + GBP.

Match page type to intent and you'll rank and convert better than with a generic page.

  • Look at what currently ranks — the SERP tells you the intent Google rewards.
  • Cluster keywords by intent and assign one page per cluster to avoid cannibalisation.

Common mistake: Targeting only high-volume keywords; long-tail phrases often convert better.
