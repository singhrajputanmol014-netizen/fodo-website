Growing a business in the US

The US is the largest and most competitive digital advertising market in the world. Costs per click are high in most categories, so waste is expensive: the brands that grow are the ones that test a clear offer, convert the traffic they already pay for, and build search visibility that compounds over time.

How customers search and buy

Buyers research across Google, YouTube, Amazon, Reddit, review sites and social platforms, often over several weeks. B2B buyers expect strong content and proof before speaking to sales; consumers expect fast sites, clear pricing and easy returns.

What usually works

Focused paid experiments with clear success measures, landing pages and CRO before scaling spend, SEO content built for problem and comparison searches, LinkedIn for B2B account lists, and analytics that ties spend to pipeline or revenue.

Time zone

India is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of US Eastern time (more for Pacific), so we work async with written updates and keep regular overlap hours for calls.

No US office

FODO has no office in the United States. All work is delivered remotely.

Rules we follow

FTC guidance on endorsements and influencer disclosure, CAN-SPAM for email, and state privacy laws such as California's where they apply.

How we work

Every engagement starts with the free growth audit: a human review of your website, Google profile, ads, social, content, follow-up and tracking. You get three priorities in order. Then we scope the work, agree the measures and report weekly in plain language. Ad spend always stays in your own accounts.
