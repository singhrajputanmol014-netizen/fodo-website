How it works

02
  1. Apply in four short stepsAbout you, your work, your portfolio, then review and send. It saves as you go.
  2. A person reviews itFODO looks at your work. If something is missing, you get a message in your application.
  3. A short call and checksFODO may call you, then checks your identity and that the work is yours.
  4. ApprovedYour FODO Pro profile is created from your application. You set your days, rates and rules.
  5. Team invitationsWhen a project needs your role, FODO invites you with the dates and your fee. Accept or decline.

What FODO handles

03
  • Clients and briefs. FODO scopes the project, prices it and builds the team.
  • Contracts. The client signs a written contract before work starts.
  • Payments. Clients pay FODO by milestone; you are paid after the client approves the work.
  • Your calendar. Accepted days are booked so you are never double-booked through FODO.
  • Your fee is clear. Team invitations show your fee before you accept.
  • Your profile stays private. Shown only to signed-in FODO clients, never as a public page.
  • Badges you earn. From your real record on FODO: answers, completed projects, repeat clients.
  • Case studies. Share finished work as case studies, published with the client's permission.
