TEARDOWN 02 · EXAMPLE

A clinic landing page that asked too much

  1. 1Answer the search. The headline matches what the patient typed.
  2. 2Show trust early. Hours, real photos and reviews above the fold.
  3. 3Cut the form. Eight fields became two.
  4. 4Make the next step obvious. One WhatsApp booking button.
