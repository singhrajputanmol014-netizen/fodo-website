How FODO gets the data

03

Your website

FODO's own reader crawls your public pages (respects robots.txt, never logs in) and runs deterministic checks.

Google Search & Maps

Real results for the searches buyers use, and local listings with ratings and review counts (via Serper).

Google PageSpeed

Lab tests plus real-user Chrome data when Google has it — shown separately, never blended.

Your Search Console (optional)

Connect it read-only to add your real clicks and impressions. Only you see it.

AI assistants

The same buyer questions to the AI engines listed in each report — FODO's own AI Visibility Score, clearly not an official one.

Your competitors

Found from those searches and listings, then read side by side with your pages.
