Growing a business in Pune

Pune combines a large student population and education sector with IT parks, automotive and manufacturing companies, a growing startup community and steady real estate demand. That mix means very different buyers: a parent choosing a coaching institute, a procurement manager shortlisting a supplier and a young professional buying a first flat all need a different path to "yes".

How customers search and buy

Students and young professionals are highly active on Instagram and YouTube; B2B buyers in manufacturing and IT rely on Google search, LinkedIn and a credible website. Marathi, Hindi and English all appear in local searches.

What usually works

Admission-season campaigns planned months ahead for education, B2B websites and SEO that explain capabilities clearly, LinkedIn for industrial and IT buyers, and local SEO for service businesses in areas like Hinjewadi, Kothrud, Baner and Wakad.

How we work with Pune teams
