Growing a business in Australia

Australian buyers respond to plain-spoken, honest marketing and are quick to tune out hype. Service businesses, trades, health and wellness, e-commerce and education all compete hard on Google and social, and local search intent is strong because customers want someone nearby.

How customers search and buy

Google dominates search; Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube drive discovery. Reviews carry a lot of weight, and suburb-level searches are common for local services. Many brands also sell into New Zealand, which has its own audience and regulations.

What usually works

Local SEO and Google Business Profiles for each location, Google Ads on high-intent service searches, honest, people-first social creative, and landing pages that make booking or buying simple on a phone.

Time zone

Sydney is 4.5 hours ahead of India (5.5 during Australian daylight saving), so our morning overlaps your afternoon. New Zealand is a further 2 hours ahead.

No Australian office

FODO has no office in Australia or New Zealand. All work is delivered remotely.

Rules we follow

The Spam Act for consent before commercial email and SMS, the Australian Consumer Law for truthful advertising claims, and the Privacy Act for personal information.
