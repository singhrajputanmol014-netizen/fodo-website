Anmol Singh — Founder, FODO

Built by someone who has spent a career understanding attention.

Anmol's background spans film and media production, assistant directing and content creation — work where attention is earned shot by shot. He then moved into business development, client relationships and marketing, before founding FODO to bring both halves together: creative craft and measurable growth.

"I started FODO because I kept watching good businesses lose to louder ones. Not better — louder. Attention is the entry fee now, and most brands are paying it badly. FODO exists to make attention count: earn it with craft, keep it with trust, and measure what it does for the business."
