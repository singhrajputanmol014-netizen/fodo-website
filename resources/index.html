General Marketing Checklist

  • One-sentence positioning written down
  • Primary audience defined
  • One business goal per quarter for marketing
  • Conversion tracking verified this month
  • Follow-up process for every enquiry
  • Monthly review scheduled with owners
  • Budget split by channel with reasons
  • Brand voice guide exists and is used

Ad Audit Checklist

  • One objective per campaign
  • Audiences do not overlap heavily
  • Negative keywords / exclusions maintained
  • Ad message matches landing page headline
  • Creative refreshed before fatigue
  • Conversion events fire once and correctly
  • Budget pacing checked weekly
  • Search terms / placements reviewed

SEO Checklist

  • Single canonical domain with HTTPS
  • Sitemap submitted and indexed pages reviewed
  • One page per core service or category
  • Titles and headings match real search phrases
  • Internal links from content to service pages
  • Images compressed, pages load fast on mobile
  • Google Business Profile accurate (if local)
  • Organic enquiries tracked, not just sessions

Landing Page Checklist

  • Headline repeats the ad promise
  • One primary call to action
  • Who it is for stated above the fold
  • Form asks only what is needed
  • Loads quickly on mobile data
  • Trust elements are specific and true
  • Thank-you state confirms next steps
  • All events tracked and tested

Social Media Audit

  • Bio states who you help and how to start
  • Link goes to a page with one job
  • Three to five content pillars defined
  • Posting cadence is sustainable
  • Comments and DMs answered within a set time
  • Profile visits to link clicks tracked
  • Highlights / pinned posts show proof and offer
  • Monthly review of what moved
