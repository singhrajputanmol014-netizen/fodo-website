AI in marketing, explained simply

AI is a powerful assistant for research, ideas, drafts, variations, analysis and automation, not a replacement for strategy, taste or facts.

Example: Use AI to turn 20 customer reviews into themes, draft 15 hook variations, summarise competitor pages you paste in, and write first drafts you then edit.

Leverage comes from good inputs (your customer data, your expertise) and human review.

  • Good uses: research synthesis, creative ideation, ad variants, repurposing, reporting, chat support with guardrails.
  • Guardrails: fact-check, brand voice review, privacy, disclose where required.

Common mistake: Mass-publishing unedited AI content, pasting confidential customer data into tools, trusting AI 'facts' without checking.

Marketing automation, explained simply

Automation sends the right message at the right time without manual work, confirmations, reminders, nurture, follow-ups.

Example: Form submitted → lead added to CRM → instant WhatsApp → salesperson task → reminder if no contact in 30 minutes.

Automate the repetitive, keep humans for judgement and relationships.

  • Start with speed-to-lead and appointment reminders, fastest ROI.
  • Respect consent and frequency.

Common mistake: Automating a messy process, you just get messy at scale.
