The FODO Growth System

03

Six stages, in order. Nothing moves to the next stage until you approve it — and every stage has a number we track.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, audience, current channels and business context.

    You get
    A clear brief that reflects your business, not a template.
    We measure
    Baseline numbers and the gaps in them.
  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We find bottlenecks, evidence gaps, funnel friction and measurement problems.

    You get
    A prioritised list of what is leaking and why.
    We measure
    Conversion by step, data quality, wasted spend.
  3. 03

    Strategize

    We decide the right audience, message, channel mix and KPIs.

    You get
    A plan you can approve, question and hold us to.
    We measure
    Agreed KPIs and thresholds.
  4. 04

    Build

    We create campaign structures, content plans, landing pages or tracking — whatever is in scope.

    You get
    Working assets reviewed before anything goes live.
    We measure
    QA pass rate, tracking verification.
  5. 05

    Launch

    We launch only after approvals, QA, tracking verification and budget agreement.

    You get
    Confidence that nothing goes live unapproved.
    We measure
    Delivery, pacing, early cost per result.
  6. 06

    Optimize

    We assess evidence, learn, iterate and report.

    You get
    Plain-language reports and a clear next decision.
    We measure
    KPI movement vs. targets, learning log.
