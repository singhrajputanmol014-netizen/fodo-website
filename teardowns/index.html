TEARDOWN 01 · EXAMPLE

A café ad that said everything — and sold nothing

  1. Hook first. The first line now says what you get, not who you are.
  2. One message. Five offers became one reason to act.
  3. Proof. A real product photo replaces shouting.
  4. One action. 'Contact us' became 'Order on WhatsApp'.

Simple ads are easier to notice, easier to trust and easier to act on.

TEARDOWN 02 · EXAMPLE

A clinic landing page that asked too much

  1. Answer the search. The headline matches what the patient typed.
  2. Show trust early. Hours, real photos and reviews above the fold.
  3. Cut the form. Eight fields became two.
  4. Make the next step obvious. One WhatsApp booking button.

Every extra field and every vague sentence costs enquiries.
