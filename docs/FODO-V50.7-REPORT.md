## Fix found while building
Role templates were seeded once: a permission added to a template in a later release (asset, report, workflow, decision, agent in v50.5 / v50.6) never reached roles that already existed on the live database. `syncTemplates()` (in `os/db.js`, run with the seed) now hands each existing role the template permissions it has not had yet, once, and remembers them in `os_template_sync`; a permission the owner removed is never put back.

## Permissions
New resources: `vendor` (view team+, manage department+), `purchase` (view / request any scope, approve team+), `integration` (view organization+, manage owner only), `subscription` (department+), `knowledge` (view / manage, publish department+), `document` (view / manage, approve team+), `inbox`, `risk` (view team+, manage department+), `incident` (view / report any scope, manage department+), `privacy` (organization+). Everyone on the team reads knowledge, drafts articles, raises purchases, keeps own documents and reports incidents. Managers approve team purchases and documents and handle their team's inbox. Operations runs vendors, knowledge, documents, inbox, risks and incidents organization-wide; finance runs vendors, purchases and subscriptions; HR publishes policies and sees the privacy desk; developers see integrations. Clients and FODO Pros get none of these.

## Jobs
`os_daily` adds: subscription renewals and integration access expiry, document expiry, article review reminders, risk reviews, privacy requests due within 3 days or late.

## Legal and credentials still needed
- A lawyer must review the privacy notice, the response time (default 30 days, owner-set), retention periods and every contract / NDA template before FODO claims DPDP compliance or uses the templates as final.
- WhatsApp (`WA_NUMBER`, `WA_TOKEN`, `WA_PHONE_ID`, `WA_APP_SECRET`) and e-signature (`ESIGN_PROVIDER`, `ESIGN_API_KEY`) show "not configured" until their secrets are set; an e-sign adapter is built only when a provider is chosen.
- Gmail in the inbox needs the owner's Google connection in Brain → Connectors with "Read Gmail".
