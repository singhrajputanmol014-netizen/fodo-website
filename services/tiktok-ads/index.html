TikTok Ads

SHORT VIDEOS. LONG MEMORY.

How we approach TikTok Ads

Short-video placements reward creative that looks native, not polished TV ads. We plan hooks for the first second, scripts that show the product working, and creator-style variations, then test them across TikTok (where available), Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Platform availability differs by country — we plan around the platforms your market really uses.

The problem

Polished brand ads feel out of place on TikTok. Without native-feeling creative and fast hook testing, spend buys views that never become customers.

TikTok — explained simply

TikTok is a short-video platform with strong discovery — but it isn't available in India (banned since 2020).

Example: For Indian audiences, the same short-video approach works on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. For audiences abroad, TikTok and TikTok Ads can be strong for consumer brands.

The skill transfers: hooks, native-looking creative, trends with a purpose.

  • Outside India: Spark Ads boost creator posts; creative refresh cadence is fast.

Common mistake: Planning a TikTok-first strategy for an India-only audience.

Creative testing — explained simply

Creative testing means running several ad concepts to learn which message, hook and format actually moves your customers.

Example: Same offer, four angles: price-transparency, pain-relief story, doctor explainer, patient testimonial. Keep the winner, iterate on it.

Test big differences (angle, hook, format) before small ones (button colour).

  • Track hook rate (3-sec views ÷ impressions), hold rate, CTR, CVR, cost per result per creative.
  • Rule of thumb: decide after each creative gets enough spend to have a meaningful number of conversions, not just clicks.

Common mistake: Testing tiny tweaks, or judging after one day.
