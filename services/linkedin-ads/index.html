B2B marketing — explained simply

B2B marketing sells to businesses: fewer, higher-value buyers, longer cycles, several decision-makers.

Example: A manufacturing supplier publishes spec-rich product pages, runs Google Search for exact product terms, posts founder insights on LinkedIn and follows up enquiries with a fast quote process.

Trust, expertise and response speed win. Lead quality over volume.

  • Channels: Search (intent), LinkedIn (targeting by role/company), content/SEO, email, events, partnerships, outbound.
  • Track: qualified meetings, pipeline value, win rate, sales cycle length.

Common mistake: Running B2C-style broad ads and judging by CPL.

Lead generation — explained simply

Lead generation is getting the right people to raise their hand — enquire, call, message or book.

Example: Search ads for high-intent keywords → focused landing page with one offer → WhatsApp + form → reply within 15 minutes → reminder sequence.

A lead system has 4 parts: traffic, offer, conversion point and follow-up. Most businesses lose more leads in follow-up than in ads.

  • Offer types: consultation, quote, audit, trial, sample, guide/checklist (lead magnet).
  • Qualify with 1–3 questions (budget, timeline, location).
  • Speed to lead: replying within minutes vs hours changes contact rates dramatically.
  • Track per source: leads → qualified → appointment → customer.

Common mistake: Measuring lead volume instead of qualified leads and closed customers.
