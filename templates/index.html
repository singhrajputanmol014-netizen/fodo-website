Monthly marketing report

The five questions every owner actually asks, plus next month's decisions, on one page.

  1. Are we getting enough? Enquiries / sales this month vs last month and vs target.
  2. What does it cost? Cost per lead or per sale, by channel.
  3. Where are we losing people? The biggest drop in the funnel and why.
  4. What is working? Top 3 ads, posts or pages, and what they have in common.
  5. Is anything broken? Tracking, forms, follow-up speed.
  6. Next month Three decisions, each with an owner and a date.
