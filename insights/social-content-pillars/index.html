How to choose social content pillars that support business goals

Content pillars are the three to five themes your social presence keeps returning to. Chosen well, they make planning easier and make your brand recognisable.

Home / Insights / How to choose social content pillars that support business goals

By Anmol Singh, Founder of FODO · 10 September 2026 · 1 min read

Content pillars are the three to five themes your social presence keeps returning to. Chosen well, they make planning easier and make your brand recognisable. Chosen badly, they are just categories for random posts.

Start from the goal, not the platform

Write down what social media should cause for the business: enquiries, bookings, store visits, trust before a sales call. Every pillar should be able to answer "how does this help that happen?"

A simple pillar structure

  • Proof — how you work, behind the scenes, process, results you can honestly show.
  • Teach — answers to the questions customers ask before buying.
  • Point of view — what you believe about your field that others do not say.
  • Offer — what you sell and how to start, stated clearly and regularly.

Test pillars against reality

Can your team produce this content every week without heroics? If a pillar needs a video crew you do not have, it will die in month two. Illustratively, a founder who can record one voice note a day has a sustainable pillar; one who needs a studio does not.

Give each pillar a job and a metric

Proof builds trust (saves, profile visits). Teach builds reach (shares). Point of view builds memory (comments). Offer builds action (link clicks, messages). Review monthly which pillar is doing its job.

Rotate, do not ration

You do not need equal volume. Lean toward what works for your goals while keeping the others alive so the feed does not become a sales page.
