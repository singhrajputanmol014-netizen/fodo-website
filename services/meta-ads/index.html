Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) — explained simply

Meta Ads show your ads in Facebook and Instagram feeds, Stories and Reels to people likely to be interested — even if they weren't searching.

Example: A café runs a Reel ad to people within 5 km showing the weekend brunch, with an offer and 'Get directions' button.

Best at creating demand. Creative is the biggest lever — the algorithm finds the audience, your ad has to stop the scroll and make a clear offer.

  • Simple structure: one campaign per objective, broad or lightly-defined audiences, 3–6 creatives testing different angles.
  • Use Pixel + Conversions API so the algorithm learns from real outcomes.
  • Instant forms are cheap but lower quality — add qualifying questions or use 'higher intent' form type; or send to WhatsApp/landing page.
  • Refresh creative when frequency rises and CTR falls (creative fatigue).

Common mistake: Boosting random posts, too many tiny ad sets, changing things daily, and optimising for clicks instead of leads/purchases.

Audience targeting & testing — explained simply

Targeting is who sees your ads; testing shows which audiences respond profitably.

Example: For a Delhi gym: radius 5 km, ages 20–45, broad vs. fitness-interest vs. lookalike of past members.

On Meta today, broad targeting with strong creative often works; on Google, keywords are the targeting.

  • Exclude existing customers from acquisition campaigns where possible.
  • Let creative do the targeting: speak to one specific person in the ad.

Common mistake: Ultra-narrow stacked interests that make delivery expensive.
