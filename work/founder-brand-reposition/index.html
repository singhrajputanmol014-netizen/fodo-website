The challenge

The insight

Being for everyone made the founder memorable to no one.

Strategy

Narrow to one audience and one promise; three content pillars; a single assessment offer.

Execution

Messaging guide, LinkedIn content calendar, landing page.

Creative

Opinionated, specific posts drawn from real client patterns (anonymised).

Channels

LinkedIn · Newsletter · Landing page

What we would measure

  • Enquiry fit
  • Newsletter growth
  • Profile to page clicks

Lesson: Specific positioning filters as much as it attracts — that is the point.
