CRO (Conversion Rate Optimisation) — explained simply

CRO is improving your site/page so more of the visitors you already have take action.

Example: Moving reviews next to the form, cutting fields from 7 to 3 and adding WhatsApp can lift enquiries without spending more on ads.

Diagnose with data (analytics, heatmaps, recordings, customer questions), then test changes one at a time.

  • Framework: clarity → relevance → trust → friction → urgency/incentive.
  • Prioritise by impact × confidence × ease (ICE).
  • Low traffic? Use qualitative research (5 customer interviews, session recordings) instead of A/B tests.

Common mistake: Redesigning everything at once on opinion, or 'testing' with too little traffic to know anything.

Experimentation — explained simply

An experiment is a structured test: one change, one metric, a decision rule — so you learn instead of guessing.

Example: Hypothesis: showing prices on the landing page will raise qualified enquiries. Change: add 'starting from ₹X'. Metric: qualified enquiry rate. Duration: 2–4 weeks. Decision: keep if qualified rate rises without lead volume collapsing.

Write the decision rule before the test so results can't be cherry-picked.

  • Prioritise with ICE (impact, confidence, ease).
  • Low traffic? Test bigger changes and use qualitative signals.

Common mistake: Changing three things at once or stopping when the first day looks good.
