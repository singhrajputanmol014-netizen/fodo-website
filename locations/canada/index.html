Growing a business in Canada

Canada's market is spread across very different regions and two official languages. A campaign that works in Toronto may need a different message in Vancouver and a French version in Quebec. Many Canadian brands also sell into the US, so planning both markets together avoids duplicated effort.

How customers search and buy

Google and YouTube lead research for most purchases, with Instagram, Facebook and TikTok driving discovery for consumer brands. Local search matters for service businesses in each city, and French-language search is essential for audiences in Quebec.

What usually works

Region-aware campaigns, French and English creative where the audience needs it, SEO and content that compounds, Google Ads on high-intent searches, and email programmes built on proper consent.

Time zone

India is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of Eastern time (Toronto, Montreal) and 12.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of Pacific time (Vancouver). We work async with agreed call windows.

No Canadian office

FODO has no office in Canada. All work is delivered remotely.

Rules we follow

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) for consent before commercial email and messages, and Quebec's French-language requirements for consumer-facing content there.
