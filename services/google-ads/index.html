Google Ads — explained simply

Google Ads puts you in front of people who are already searching for what you offer (plus YouTube, Display and Shopping).

Example: A dentist bids on 'dentist near me', 'root canal cost Dwarka' and sends clicks to a fast landing page with a call button and booking form.

Best at capturing existing demand (intent). Results depend on keyword choice, match types, negative keywords, ad relevance, landing page and conversion tracking.

  • Structure: campaign per goal/location → tight ad groups by theme → responsive search ads with strong headlines.
  • Search terms report weekly → add negatives. Start with phrase/exact match on a small budget.
  • Track calls, form fills and WhatsApp clicks as conversions; import offline sales if you can.
  • Performance Max can scale e-commerce but needs good feeds, creatives and conversion data — guard brand terms.

Common mistake: Broad keywords with no negatives (paying for 'free', 'jobs', 'course'), sending traffic to the homepage, and no conversion tracking.

CPC (Cost per Click) — explained simply

CPC is what you pay on average for each click.

Formula: CPC = ad spend ÷ clicks

Example: ₹10,000 → 500 clicks → CPC = ₹20.

Driven by auction competition, relevance and audience. On its own it says little — a ₹80 click that converts at 10% beats a ₹10 click that converts at 0.5%.

  • High-intent keywords ("dentist near me") cost more per click but usually convert far better.
  • Improve relevance (ad ↔ keyword ↔ landing page) to lower CPC on Google; better creative engagement helps on Meta.

Common mistake: Switching channels or keywords just to get cheaper clicks without checking cost per customer.
