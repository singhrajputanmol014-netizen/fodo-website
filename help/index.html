Payments & refunds

If an X-Ray cannot run because the website cannot be read at all, the credit comes back automatically. If you were charged but did not get your plan or credits, or were charged twice, write to us within 7 days and that charge is refunded in full. If you have not used any X-Ray from a purchase, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of buying it. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days. Used X-Rays and partly used months are not refunded. A person at FODO approves every refund — the assistant cannot promise one. Open →
Payments are confirmed with Cashfree on our server, usually within a minute. Open your account page — it re-checks the last payment. If it still shows nothing after 10 minutes, or the money was taken twice, open a support ticket with the time of payment and we will fix or refund it. Open →
Payments go through Cashfree Payments (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets). FODO never sees your card or UPI details. The payment page needs a 10-digit Indian mobile number for the receipt. Open →
Every payment appears with its amount and date in your account under receipts. For a GST invoice with your company details, open a ticket with your GSTIN and billing name. Open →
