Growing a business in the UAE

The UAE is one of the most competitive digital ad markets in the region: a young, multinational, mobile-first population, very high social media use, and categories such as real estate, hospitality, beauty, education and e-commerce where everyone advertises. Standing out takes a sharp offer, premium-feeling creative and fast follow-up.

How customers search and buy

Audiences are multilingual — Arabic and English are essential, and Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam and Tagalog matter for some segments. WhatsApp is the default way to enquire, and Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube all carry real buying intent. Ramadan, Eid and the winter tourist season change both demand and ad costs.

What usually works

Separate Arabic and English creative rather than direct translation, WhatsApp-first lead flows with quick response, Google Ads for high-intent real estate, education and service searches, and seasonal planning around Ramadan, Eid and the cooler months.

Time zone

Gulf Standard Time is 1.5 hours behind India, so our working day overlaps almost completely with yours.

No local office

FODO has no office in the UAE. Strategy, campaigns and reporting are delivered remotely; meetings are on video.

Rules we check

Paid influencer and advertising content in the UAE is regulated by the UAE Media Council, and personal data falls under the UAE's data protection law. We check creator licensing and consent requirements before any campaign.

Services we usually start with

  • Meta Ads — Where your customers scroll. Be there.
  • Google Ads — Be the answer when they search.
  • TikTok Ads — Short videos. Long memory.
  • Social Media Management — Show up. Stand out. Stay consistent.
  • Landing Pages — One page. One job.

Every engagement starts with the free growth audit: a human review of your website, Google profile, ads, social, content, follow-up and tracking. You get three priorities in order.
