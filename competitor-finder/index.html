          How to use it

          01
          1. Pick a modeLocal businesses near an address, or competitors of a brand, startup or industry.
          2. Start typingSuggestions appear for places, business types and brands as you type.
          3. Run a few searchesEvery result is added to one sheet, mix cities, categories and brands.
          4. Download the sheetExcel, CSV or paste straight into Google Sheets, then plan where you can win.

          Local listings come from OpenStreetMap, Geoapify and Google Maps (when connected), searched together; brand data comes from Wikidata and AI suggestions that are checked before they are shown as confirmed. Google Maps details are shown here only and are not put in the download, as Google’s terms require, the sheet keeps the Google Maps link. Details belong to each business and may be out of date, check the live listing or website before acting. FODO does not keep your searches; your sheet stays in this browser. On a phone, downloads open a save/share panel so the file lands in Files.
