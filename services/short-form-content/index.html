Short-form video (Reels/Shorts) — explained simply

Short-form video is 15–60 second vertical content — the main way new people discover businesses on Instagram and YouTube today.

Example: Hook (0–2s): 'Stop paying ₹80 per click until you check this.' → 3 quick points → proof → CTA.

The first 2 seconds decide everything. Clear, specific, useful or entertaining beats polished.

  • Structure: hook → problem → insight → proof → CTA.
  • Batch-shoot 8–12 reels in one session using a script list.
  • Track 3-sec hold, average watch %, shares, saves, profile visits.

Common mistake: Long intros, logos first, trending audio with no message.

YouTube — explained simply

YouTube is the world's second-biggest search engine — great for education, demos and trust, with Shorts for discovery.

Example: A coaching institute publishes 'how to solve' videos, Shorts clipped from them, and runs YouTube ads to retarget viewers.

Long-form builds deep trust; Shorts drive reach. Titles/thumbnails decide clicks.

  • Plan around searchable questions.
  • Repurpose one long video into 5–10 Shorts.

Common mistake: Uploading without a topic strategy; ignoring thumbnails.
