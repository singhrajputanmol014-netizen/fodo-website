Growing a business in the UK

UK buyers are well informed and quick to notice exaggeration. In crowded categories, professional services, e-commerce, health and wellness, hospitality, education, growth usually comes from clearer positioning, stronger search visibility and better conversion rather than simply spending more on ads.

How customers search and buy

Google remains the main research channel for most purchases, supported by reviews, comparison sites and social proof on Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. Local intent is strong for service businesses ("near me", town and postcode searches).

What usually works

SEO and content that answer real comparison questions, Google Ads on tightly matched searches, Meta and TikTok creative that feels native rather than salesy, conversion-focused landing pages and clean, consent-aware tracking.

Time zone

India is 5.5 hours ahead of the UK in winter (GMT) and 4.5 hours ahead in summer (BST), so we overlap with your full morning and early afternoon.

No UK office

FODO has no office in the UK. All work is delivered remotely with video calls and written weekly reports.

Rules we follow

UK GDPR and PECR for consent, cookies and email marketing, and the CAP Code for advertising claims and influencer disclosure.
