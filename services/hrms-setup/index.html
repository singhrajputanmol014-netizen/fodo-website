PEOPLE OPS WITHOUT THE PAPERWORK.

How we approach HRMS Setup

When a team passes a certain size, registers, chats and spreadsheets stop working. We map what you actually need, select or configure an HRMS that fits your size and budget, migrate employee data cleanly, set role-based access, and train managers so the system is used, not bypassed. You own the subscription; we are tied to no vendor.

Attendance in registers, leave requests on WhatsApp, salary inputs in three spreadsheets, and nobody with one clear view of the team.

Marketing automation, explained simply

Automation sends the right message at the right time without manual work, confirmations, reminders, nurture, follow-ups.

Example: Form submitted → lead added to CRM → instant WhatsApp → salesperson task → reminder if no contact in 30 minutes.

Automate the repetitive, keep humans for judgement and relationships.

  • Start with speed-to-lead and appointment reminders, fastest ROI.
  • Respect consent and frequency.

Common mistake: Automating a messy process, you just get messy at scale.

CRM & follow-up, explained simply

A CRM keeps every lead, conversation and next step in one place so nobody falls through the cracks.

Example: Lead arrives → auto WhatsApp in 1 min → salesperson calls within 15 min → status 'contacted' → follow-ups on day 1, 3, 7 → won/lost with reason.

Most businesses lose more money in slow, inconsistent follow-up than in ads.

  • Define stages and an owner for each lead.
  • Track speed-to-lead, contact rate, show rate, close rate.
  • Feed outcomes back to ads so platforms optimise for buyers.

Common mistake: Leads living in WhatsApp chats and notebooks; no 'lost reason' tracking.
