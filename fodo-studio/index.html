Install with one line

03

The easiest way on a computer: paste one line, press Enter, and FODO Studio downloads, checks the file, installs and opens by itself. Nothing to double-click, so Windows Smart App Control and macOS do not block it.

WINDOWS 10 / 11

Windows: PowerShell

  1. Press Start, type PowerShell, press Enter (no admin needed).
  2. Paste this line and press Enter:
irm https://www.fodo.co.in/downloads/win-install.ps1 | iex

Keep the window open: 5 to 15 minutes the first time (Python, FFmpeg and the local AI model come from Windows' own installer, winget). FODO Studio opens when it says Done.

MAC · MACOS 15.0+

Mac: Terminal

  1. Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  2. Paste this line and press Enter:
curl -fsSL https://www.fodo.co.in/downloads/mac-install.sh | bash

No Mac password and no Open Anyway needed. Keep Terminal open: 5 to 15 minutes the first time. FODO Studio opens when it says Done.

When the app opens, type your email and press Request access: FODO emails your key.

Remove FODO Studio (uninstall with one line too)

Windows: Settings > Apps > FODO Studio > Uninstall, or paste this in PowerShell:

irm https://www.fodo.co.in/downloads/win-uninstall.ps1 | iex

Mac: paste this in Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://www.fodo.co.in/downloads/mac-uninstall.sh | bash

It closes FODO Studio and removes the app and its icons. It asks before deleting your videos and exports (press Enter to keep them). Python, FFmpeg and Ollama are separate apps and stay. Your key stays on your account, so you can install again any time.
