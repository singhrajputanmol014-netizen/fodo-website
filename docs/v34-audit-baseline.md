## Critical findings at baseline

1. **Stock oversell:** stock was read at checkout and decremented later. Two buyers could buy the last item.
2. **Coupon over-use:** the `max_uses` check was a SELECT followed by a separate UPDATE, and the coupon was consumed even when the payment page failed.
3. **Duplicate order numbers:** order numbers came from `MAX(number)+1` with no unique index.
4. **Refunds:** "refunded" was just a status. No money moved and no gateway record was kept.
5. **Password-protected pages:** used unsalted SHA-256 of `password|siteId`, with no attempt limit and no session.
6. **Builder:** no tablet / mobile overrides, saving conflicts used a `confirm()` box, a network drop threw instead of showing "offline".
7. **SEO:** 7 errors + 30 warnings. `/website-builder/` was missing from the sitemap and had no H1; `/owner-brain/` was audited as a public page; 13 Growth Lab descriptions were too short.
8. **Leads:** contact / audit / call forms only opened WhatsApp, so nothing was stored.
9. **Settings:** the booking link, GA4 / Pixel IDs and the founding counter needed a code deploy to change.
