Social media (Instagram & co.) — explained simply

Social media builds familiarity and trust at scale — the goal is attention that turns into enquiries, not just likes.

Example: A boutique posts 3 Reels a week (styling ideas, behind-the-scenes, customer try-ons with permission), answers DMs fast and runs a small retargeting ad to engagers.

Consistency + useful/entertaining content + a clear next step (DM, WhatsApp, link) beats random daily posting.

  • Content pillars (education, proof, story, behind-the-scenes, offer) keep you consistent.
  • Measure saves, shares, profile visits, DMs and link clicks — not just likes.
  • Reels/short video for reach; carousels for saves; Stories for warm audience and offers.

Common mistake: Chasing followers and trends unrelated to the business; no CTA; ignoring DMs.

Content calendar — explained simply

A content calendar plans what you'll post, where and when — so you stay consistent and every post has a purpose.

Example: Mon: tip (education) · Wed: behind-the-scenes · Fri: customer story/proof · Sun: offer or FAQ. Batch-create on one day a week.

Consistency beats intensity. Plan by pillars and funnel stage.

  • Mix: 40% education, 25% proof, 20% story/BTS, 15% offer.
  • Review monthly: double down on formats that drive DMs/enquiries.

Common mistake: Planning 30 random 'days' (Monday motivation…) with no business link.
