The marketing funnel — explained simply

The funnel is the path from stranger to customer: traffic → landing page → lead → qualified lead → appointment → sale → repeat → referral.

Example: 10,000 people see an ad → 200 click → 20 enquire → 8 qualify → 4 book → 2 buy. Each arrow is a conversion rate you can improve.

Find the leakiest step before spending more on the top. Lots of clicks + few leads = page/offer problem. Lots of leads + few sales = qualification/follow-up problem.

  • TOFU (awareness) content, MOFU (consideration: proof, comparisons), BOFU (offer, urgency, risk reversal).
  • Track each step weekly in one simple sheet.

Common mistake: Pouring more ad money into a funnel that leaks at the bottom.

On-page SEO — explained simply

On-page SEO is shaping each page — title, headings, content, links — to match what the searcher wants.

Example: Title: 'Root Canal Treatment in Dwarka — Painless, Same-Day | Dr X Dental'. H1 matches, content answers cost, pain, recovery and FAQs.

Match intent, answer completely, make it scannable, and link to related pages.

  • Write for the question behind the keyword; include original details (prices, process, photos, expert input).
  • Internal links from strong pages pass relevance and authority.

Common mistake: Keyword stuffing, or one page trying to rank for ten different services.
