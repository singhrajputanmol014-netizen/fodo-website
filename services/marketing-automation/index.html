Marketing automation — explained simply

Automation sends the right message at the right time without manual work — confirmations, reminders, nurture, follow-ups.

Example: Form submitted → lead added to CRM → instant WhatsApp → salesperson task → reminder if no contact in 30 minutes.

Automate the repetitive, keep humans for judgement and relationships.

  • Start with speed-to-lead and appointment reminders — fastest ROI.
  • Respect consent and frequency.

Common mistake: Automating a messy process — you just get messy at scale.

WhatsApp marketing — explained simply

WhatsApp is where many Indian customers prefer to talk — perfect for fast replies, appointment reminders, catalogues and follow-ups with consent.

Example: Click-to-WhatsApp ad → auto-reply with menu → human replies within 10 minutes → booking confirmation → reminder the day before → review request after.

Speed and helpfulness convert. Use the official WhatsApp Business app/API, opt-in, and relevant messages only.

  • Use labels/CRM stages: new, contacted, quoted, booked, won, lost.
  • Quick replies for FAQs; catalogue for products; templates (API) for reminders.
  • Measure response time, booking rate from chats and revenue per conversation.

Common mistake: Spamming broadcast lists (risk of number bans), slow replies, no follow-up system.
