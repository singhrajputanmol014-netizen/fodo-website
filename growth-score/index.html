How this works

Each answer maps to a 0–100 value. Seven categories are derived: Brand (social 60% + content 40%), Traffic (traffic 70% + SEO 30%), Content, SEO, Paid Media (paid 70% + budget 30%), Conversion (leads 60% + tracking 40%), Analytics (tracking). Overall = Brand 10% + the other six at 15% each, clamped to 0–100. "I don't know" scores low deliberately. Nothing is crawled; this is a self-assessment, not an audit.
